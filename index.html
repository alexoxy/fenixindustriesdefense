Fábrica de Munições Fenix

Portuguese enterprise. Global industrial ambition.

Fábrica de Munições Fenix is a Portugal-based business initiative dedicated to developing industrial-scale small-calibre ammunition manufacturing. The initiative combines private investment, specialized industrial capabilities and a long-term commitment to European supply-chain resilience.

The business is being structured as a joint venture with a global defense industry partner ranked among the world's Top 25, bringing together Portuguese industrial development and international manufacturing expertise.

Industrial location

Ponte de Sor Industrial Zone.

The Fenix industrial facility is planned for the industrial zone of Ponte de Sor, Portugal. The location offers a basis for industrial infrastructure, logistics access and qualified employment as the initiative advances through its development, investment and licensing stages.
