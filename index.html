The initiative

Building strategic manufacturing capacity in Portugal.

Fábrica de Munições Fenix is a Portuguese industrial initiative in the investment-structuring stage, focused on creating modern manufacturing capacity for the defense sector. The project is designed around industrial reliability, technological know-how, qualified employment and long-term development.

Portugal's Atlantic position, access to European markets and industrial talent provide a compelling foundation for a manufacturing platform with an international outlook.

Project recognition
PIN 331

Recognised investment initiative.

The Fenix initiative has been assigned Project of Potential National Interest (PIN) number 331 in Portugal. This status reflects the project's strategic investment framework; it does not imply that the facility is already operating or that all permits and financing have been completed.

International outlook

A future joint venture with a global defense industry partner.

The project is being structured with the objective of establishing a joint venture involving an international defense industry partner. This model is intended to combine Portuguese industrial investment with global technological experience, manufacturing expertise and access to international supply chains.

The planned partnership forms part of the project's investment development process and remains subject to final agreements and the applicable approvals.
